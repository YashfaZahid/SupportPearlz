# Pearlz Home Systems — Warranty Policy
**Document type:** policy  
**Version:** 2026.1  
**Last updated:** 2026-01-15  
**Applies to:** AquaPearl 300, AquaPearl 500 Pro, AeroPearl Mini, and discontinued models under extended warranty

## 1. Warranty periods by product line

| Product line | Standard warranty | What starts the clock |
|--------------|-------------------|------------------------|
| AquaPearl 300 | 24 months | Invoice / purchase date (not delivery date) |
| AquaPearl 500 Pro | 24 months | Invoice / purchase date (not delivery date) |
| AeroPearl Mini | 18 months | Invoice / purchase date (not delivery date) |
| Discontinued AquaPearl 200 / AeroPearl Classic | Extended warranty only if purchased; see §6 | As stated on extended-warranty certificate |

## 2. What is covered

The standard warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship for:

- Purifier / appliance **body** and chassis
- **Pump** and motor assemblies
- **Electronics**, control board, and indicator systems
- Factory-sealed internal plumbing of the unit itself

Coverage means repair or replacement of the defective part or unit at Pearlz’s discretion, at no charge for parts covered under this policy when the claim is valid.

## 3. Exclusions

### 3.1 Consumables

**Filter cartridges** (sediment pre-filter, carbon block, RO membrane, remineralisation cartridge, and HEPA/carbon packs for AeroPearl) are classified as **consumables** and are **excluded** from the standard warranty. Consumables must be purchased separately.

### 3.2 Other exclusions

The warranty does **not** cover:

- Physical damage, cracks, drops, or impact (including cracked housing)
- Damage from unauthorised repair or opening by a non-Pearlz technician
- Commercial or continuous industrial use of a household unit
- Damage caused by water quality outside the stated operating range (see product manual)
- Property damage to cabinets, flooring, or surroundings caused by leaks (the unit’s covered parts may still be assessed separately; property remediation is not promised by this policy)
- Consumable depletion, taste preferences, or aesthetic wear

## 4. How the period is calculated

The warranty period runs from the **invoice / purchase date** printed on the sales invoice, **not** from the delivery date. Keep the invoice; it is required for claims.

## 5. Transferability

The remaining warranty period may transfer to a new owner **once**, if the original invoice and a transfer form are submitted within 30 days of resale. Consumables remain excluded after transfer.

## 6. Extended warranty for discontinued models

AquaPearl 200 and AeroPearl Classic may carry a separately purchased extended warranty of up to 12 additional months. Terms are those printed on the extended-warranty certificate. No extended warranty is implied for current models unless purchased as a named add-on SKU.

## 7. How to claim

To start a warranty claim you must contact Pearlz Support with:

1. Proof of purchase (invoice showing date and model)
2. Serial number of the unit
3. A short description of the fault and any error codes

**The complete claim procedure, required photographs, service SLAs, and authorised service routing are described in the Service & Maintenance Agreement** — this warranty policy does not duplicate that checklist. Unauthorised repair during the warranty period is an exclusion under §3.2.
