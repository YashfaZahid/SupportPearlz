# Pearlz Home Systems — Shipping Policy
**Version:** 2026.1  
**Last updated:** 2026-02-01

## 1. Delivery timelines by region tier

| Tier | Examples | Standard delivery window |
|------|----------|---------------------------|
| Metro | Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad | 1–3 working days |
| Secondary city | Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar, Quetta, Rawalpindi | 3–5 working days |
| Remote | Skardu, Gilgit, and other listed remote areas | **5–7 working days** |

Working days exclude Sundays and public holidays.

## 2. Cut-off for same-day dispatch

Orders confirmed and paid before **14:00 Pakistan Standard Time** on a working day are eligible for same-day dispatch from the serving warehouse. Later orders dispatch the next working day.

## 3. Free shipping threshold

Orders of **PKR 15,000** or more (before tax) receive free standard shipping within Pakistan. Below that threshold, a flat shipping fee applies as shown at checkout.

## 4. Express and next-day delivery

**Next-day delivery** is offered only in **Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad**, and only when the order is placed before the 14:00 cut-off and stock is local.

Next-day delivery is **not** offered to secondary or remote areas, including **Skardu**. The documentation does **not** describe a purchasable exception that converts remote-area windows to next-day.

## 5. Fragile items

Water purifiers and air purifiers ship in reinforced cartons with edge protectors. Customers should refuse delivery if the outer carton is crushed and note damage on the courier POD.

## 6. Failed delivery attempts

The courier makes up to **two** delivery attempts. After two failures the parcel returns to the warehouse; re-delivery may incur an additional fee.

## 7. International shipping

International shipping is **not** currently offered.

## 8. Tracking

A tracking link is emailed when the parcel is handed to the courier. Tracking status is informational; delivery windows above remain authoritative.
