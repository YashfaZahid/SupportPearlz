# Pearlz Home Systems — Privacy Policy
**Version:** 2026.1  
**Last updated:** 2026-01-10

## 1. Data collected at purchase

We collect name, shipping address, phone number, email, payment reference (not full card PAN — handled by the payment processor), and order contents.

## 2. Mobile app and water-usage telemetry

If you use the Pearlz app with a connected AquaPearl 500 Pro, we may collect:

- Device serial and firmware version  
- Filter-life estimates and alert codes  
- Approximate daily volume dispensed (litres), stored as telemetry  

Telemetry is used for support diagnostics and product improvement. It is **not** sold to data brokers.

## 3. Retention periods

| Data class | Retention |
|------------|-----------|
| Order and invoice records | 7 years (tax / warranty) |
| Support tickets | 3 years after closure |
| App telemetry | 24 months rolling |
| Marketing email preference logs | Until unsubscribe + 12 months |

## 4. Third parties

We share data only as needed with:

- Courier partners (name, address, phone for delivery)  
- Payment processors (payment tokens / references)  
- Authorised service technicians (serial, fault description)

## 5. User rights

You may request access, correction, deletion (subject to legal retention), or export of personal data by emailing privacy@pearlzhome.example. We respond within 30 days.

## 6. Cookies

The online store uses essential cookies for cart and login, and optional analytics cookies that can be declined.
