# Pearlz Frequently Asked Questions
**Version:** 2026.1  
*Phrased in customer language for semantic retrieval testing.*

## Ordering & account

**Q: How do I place an order?**  
A: Order on the Pearlz online store or through an authorised retail partner. Keep your invoice for warranty.

**Q: Can I change my shipping address after ordering?**  
A: Yes, if the order has not yet dispatched. Email support@pearlzhome.example with your order number.

**Q: Do you price-match other websites?**  
A: Pearlz does not operate a formal price-match programme. Promotional prices are listed in the Pricing Guide.

**Q: I never got a confirmation email.**  
A: Check spam, then contact support with the payment reference. We do not resend passwords by chat.

## Delivery

**Q: How long does delivery take to Karachi?**  
A: Metro areas including Karachi are typically 1–3 working days under the Shipping Policy.

**Q: What about Skardu or other remote places?**  
A: Remote-area delivery is 5–7 working days. See the Shipping Policy for tiers and next-day limits.

**Q: Is next-day delivery available everywhere?**  
A: No. Next-day is only for Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad when cut-off and stock rules are met.

**Q: The courier says they tried to deliver but I was home.**  
A: Couriers make up to two attempts; ask Support to open a delivery investigation with your tracking ID.

## Installation

**Q: Can I install the AquaPearl myself?**  
A: Yes if you follow the Installation Guide. Incorrect self-installation that damages fittings can void warranty for that damage.

**Q: Do I need a plumber?**  
A: Professional installation can be booked; see the Pricing Guide for the service SKU and the Installation Guide for what is included.

**Q: Why do I have to flush the system after installing filters?**  
A: Flushing clears carbon fines and manufacturing residue. Skipping it often causes temporary bad taste. Steps are in the Installation Guide.

## Filters & maintenance

**Q: How often do I change the sediment filter?**  
A: On AquaPearl 500 Pro, the sediment pre-filter is typically every 3–6 months depending on water quality; see the product manual intervals.

**Q: My machine beeps three times — is it broken?**  
A: Three short beeps usually mean error E-03 (sediment pre-filter saturation). Replace the pre-filter and hold RESET for five seconds. Details in Troubleshooting and the product manual.

**Q: Are filters covered by warranty?**  
A: No. Filters are consumables excluded from the standard warranty.

**Q: Can I use third-party filter cartridges?**  
A: Only Pearlz-compatible cartridges listed in the Pricing Guide are supported. Unauthorised cartridges may void warranty for related failures.

## Warranty & returns

**Q: How long is the warranty on the AquaPearl 500 Pro?**  
A: 24 months from the invoice date. See the Warranty Policy.

**Q: I bought it online — does warranty start when it arrives?**  
A: No. It starts on the invoice / purchase date, not delivery.

**Q: What’s your return window?**  
A: 14 calendar days from delivery for eligible returns. See Refund & Returns Policy for opened vs unopened rules.

**Q: Can I return an opened unit?**  
A: Possibly, if unused and complete; a restocking fee may apply. Used/installed units are not returnable for refund.

**Q: My friend said the warranty is 5 years.**  
A: That is incorrect for standard cover. Documented periods are in the Warranty Policy (24 months for AquaPearl 500 Pro).

## App & account

**Q: The app won’t connect to my purifier.**  
A: Enable Bluetooth, confirm you selected AquaPearl 500 Pro, and retry pairing. See Troubleshooting symptom “App will not pair”.

**Q: Does the app listen to my conversations?**  
A: No. The app may collect filter telemetry as described in the Privacy Policy — not microphone audio for advertising.

## Pricing

**Q: How much is “the filter”?**  
A: There are multiple filter SKUs (sediment, carbon, RO membrane, bundles). See the Pricing Guide and specify the product line.

**Q: Is the AeroPearl Mini cheaper than AquaPearl?**  
A: List and promotional prices are in the Pricing Guide CSV; AeroPearl Mini is a different product category (air).
