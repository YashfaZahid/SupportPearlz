# Planted seams and gaps (author scratch — keep)

This file is for examiners and evaluation design. It is **not** customer-facing knowledge.
Loaders should skip files whose names start with `_`.

## Seams (multi-document answers)

1. **Warranty claim procedure** — Warranty policy says how to start a claim but refers to the Service & Maintenance Agreement for the full document checklist and SLA. Claim steps exist **only** in the service agreement.
2. **First-run flushing after filter change** — Customers describe “weird taste after changing the filter”; the remedy (flush procedure) lives in the **Installation Guide**, not Troubleshooting.
3. **Remote shipping to Skardu** — Timelines and next-day exclusions for remote areas live **only** in Shipping Policy (FAQ may point vaguely to shipping).

## Gaps (must refuse — do not fill later)

1. Student discounts / education pricing.
2. NSF / WQA / third-party health certifications for filters.
3. Buy-now-pay-later / instalment payment plans.
